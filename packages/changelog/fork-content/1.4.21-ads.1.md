## Activity and settings

- See conversation count, hours transcribed, active days, weekly streak, and milestones in **Your stats**
- Choose where notes and recordings are stored, or open their current folder directly
- Control transcript, summary, recording-status, and upcoming-event notifications separately
- Choose and preview optional completion sounds for finished background work

## Providers and recording

- Verify API keys before saving provider settings while preserving existing setup through temporary connection failures
- Keep assigned speaker names across recordings made after pausing and resuming capture
- Recover cleanly when the desktop renderer or system-audio capture fails
