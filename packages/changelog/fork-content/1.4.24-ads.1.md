## Transcription and recording

- Recover stalled live transcription while audio capture keeps running, with cleaner handoffs between meetings
- Honor cancellation of post-meeting transcription and keep saved transcripts when post-processing returns incomplete text
- Preserve all requested languages in mixed-language transcription and re-transcribe stereo recordings through mono-only providers
- Use Bluetooth microphones on macOS reliably and keep other meeting apps running while Anarlog is open
- Connect to local AI servers and save custom transcription providers that do not publish a model list

## Notes and meetings

- Start summaries from the live transcript, then refresh them when the final transcript is ready
- Remember speaker names across restarts and preserve assignments through transcript refinement
- Edit transcript text directly, save with Command-S, and copy or reassign a speaker from a selection
- Undo applied memo and summary edits without losing earlier undo history
- Skip scheduled auto-starts while another meeting is already recording

## Calendar and files

- Keep notes and ignored-event choices attached to recurring Apple Calendar meetings when an occurrence is rescheduled, without creating duplicates
- Choose where desktop exports are saved and open the folder picker beside note header actions
